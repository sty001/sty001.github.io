Beyond technology, here are some small tweaks that make everyday life smoother.

## Workstation

- Raise the monitor to eye level — your neck will thank you
- Tidy up the cables and half of your bad mood disappears
- A good desk lamp is worth more than any peripheral

## Productivity Tools

- Take notes with "cards + bidirectional links" — knowledge weaves its own web
- Keep task management to just two columns: "do today" and "later" — don't over-plan
- Automate repetitive actions: if a script can do it, never click it by hand

## A Few Takeaways

- Tools are the means, not the end; don't hide in configuration while avoiding real output
- Declutter regularly: the same goes for files, subscriptions, and to-dos

> Only when life is tidied up does the brain have room for what matters more.
