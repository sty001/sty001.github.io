## Content Formats

- **Study notes** — Each column opens with an overview article, then fills in sub-topics one by one; series articles carry numbered titles (e.g. "XX Series 03")
- **Project retrospectives** — Boards I have built, code I have written, pitfalls I have hit — together with the verification process
- **Tools & resources** — Toolchains, references and personal projects, collected in the [About page](/about/){:lang="zh"} and the documentation hub below

## Quick Start

### 1. Want to learn a direction systematically

Start from the **overview article** of the corresponding column (click the column name in the matrix above); every overview provides a map of that direction's sub-topics.

### 2. Looking for a specific article

Use the **search box** in the top bar, or browse the [tags page](/tags/){:lang="zh"} and the [archives](/archives/){:lang="zh"} chronologically.

### 3. About me / Contact

See the [About page](/about/){:lang="zh"}: personal profile, roadmap and contact details. You can leave a comment under any article (Giscus powered).
